Generative art

Between November 2021 and December 2023 I made 46 generative projects on fx(hash). Almost all of them are written in p5.js, by hand, line by line.

This is work from before the age of AI. There was no assistant to ask, so each piece was a real challenge: figuring out the maths, the structure and the bugs on my own. Go through them in order and you can see how my work progressed.

One of them, Kolorfon, also exists as a physical installation. Here I built a digital version of it.
